## 01 | HAL NASHIK

**THE EXPERIENCE THAT MADE IT REAL**

At HAL Nashik, aerospace engineering became real for me. For the first time, I saw fighter aircraft up close, watched a Sukhoi engine roar at full afterburner, and sat inside an Su-30 while seeing how design, structures, avionics and overhaul come together in the real world.

---

## 02 | L&T DEFENCE

**CURIOSITY BECAME INVESTIGATION**

The work began with a VTOL UAV research problem and grew beyond the initial requirement into a database of more than 170 aircraft platforms. Using Python and SQL, I explored relationships between platform characteristics and learned to follow curiosity beyond the task initially given.

---

## 03 | GARUD

**FROM AN IDEA TO THE REAL WORLD**

GARUD was where a larger engineering challenge was carried from concept toward a physical system. Design it, analyse it, build it, and see what happens when theory meets reality.

---

## 04 | IIT KANPUR

**CHOOSING THE HARDER ROOM**

Coming to IIT Kanpur meant choosing to start again in a more demanding environment. I wanted to be surrounded by harder problems, people who knew more than me, and work that would force me to keep learning.

---

## 05 | COMPUTATIONAL RESEARCH

**GOING DEEPER**

My current work is taking me deeper into computational research. Before extending an existing CFD workflow, I am learning to trace the system, establish a trustworthy baseline, validate it against reference data and understand the problem before trying to change it.

It is the same principle that has followed me through every stage of this journey: the more you learn, the more there is still left to understand.

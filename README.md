# Engineering begins where the obvious answer ends.

I am an aerospace engineer working across physical systems and high-fidelity computational engineering. My engineering approach is simple: understand the system, question the immediate requirement, build, validate against evidence, and improve the result. 

My work bridges physical aerospace hardware and advanced numerical methods—from designing thrust-vector-controlled rockets and analyzing defense platforms, to developing GPU-accelerated finite-volume solvers, training Physics-Informed Neural Networks, and running Large Eddy Simulations of turbulent flows.

---

## SELECTED ENGINEERING WORK

### [M.Tech Thesis: High-Fidelity LES of Contrail Formation](https://github.com/Tanish0224/les-contrail-formation)
**Ongoing Research | Computational Fluid Dynamics & HPC**  
Simulating aircraft contrail formation by modeling turbulent jet-vortex interactions and ice microphysics using the Incompact3D framework.
*   **Current Progress:** Established a temporal turbulent-jet baseline and evaluated dynamic Smagorinsky versus explicit high-order low-pass filtering for Large Eddy Simulation (LES).
*   **Planned Extension:** Developing the numerical framework for Lagrangian passive and active particle tracking to accurately model ice microphysics.

### [GPU-Accelerated Compressible Flow Solver](https://github.com/Tanish0224/gpu-accelerated-cfd)
**High-Performance Computing | CUDA C & CuPy**  
Developed a high-performance 2D compressible finite-volume solver (MUSCL, HLL Riemann) that directly restructures memory access for GPU acceleration.
*   **Technical Approach:** Implemented custom ultra and tiled kernels using CuPy and CUDA C (RawKernel). 
*   **Optimization:** Leveraged shared-memory temporal blocking and double-buffering to maximize data reuse and kernel execution efficiency.

### [Physics-Informed FNO for 3D Flow-Field Extrapolation](https://github.com/Tanish0224/physics-informed-fno-bladenet)
**Scientific Machine Learning | Neural Operators**  
Evaluated a continuity-regularized Fourier Neural Operator (PI-FNO) for predicting steady-state aerodynamic flow fields over unseen 3D blade geometries.
*   **Results:** Implemented an explicit discrete spatial divergence penalty during training, reducing local continuity residuals by 98.5% across held-out geometric extrapolations.

### [Project GARUD: Thrust-Vector-Controlled Model Rocket](https://github.com/Tanish0224/garud-tvc-rocket)
**Aerospace Hardware & Systems Integration**  
Collaborated within a four-member team to design and build a functioning TVC rocket system, bringing it from concept through fabrication and physical integration.
*   **Leadership & Ownership:** Led the mechanical and CAD design of the thrust-vector-control mechanism and the deployable landing-leg assembly.

### [VTOL UAV Performance Analysis](https://github.com/Tanish0224/vtol-uav-performance-analysis)
**Data-Driven Aerospace Systems Investigation**  
Compiled publicly available aircraft specifications to study design trade-offs, payload relationships, and configuration trends in VTOL platforms.
*   **Execution:** Built and queried a structured MySQL database containing approximately 195 usable VTOL UAV records (from over 200 researched platforms) to extract statistical design patterns.

## ENGINEERING TRAJECTORY

The tools I use have evolved as the complexity of the problems has increased, but my fundamental engineering mindset remains the same.

*   **Aerospace Hardware & Physical Systems:** My foundation was built on physical hardware. Through university rocketry teams and Project GARUD, I learned that digital designs must survive manufacturing tolerances, assembly constraints, and physical reality. Leading the mechanical design for TVC mechanisms taught me how individual components integrate into a functioning system.
*   **Systems Engineering & Data:** Exposure to aircraft systems at HAL Nashik and defense research at L&T Defence shifted my perspective toward system-level interaction. I began using Python and SQL to build databases (like the VTOL UAV study) to investigate entire design spaces quantitatively rather than just fulfilling a single requirement.
*   **Computational Engineering & Fluid Mechanics:** Seeking deeper explanations for physical behaviors, I moved toward computational methods. At IIT Kanpur, I focus on high-fidelity fluid mechanics, HPC, and scientific machine learning. I write GPU-accelerated numerical solvers, train Physics-Informed Neural Networks to enforce mass conservation, and run high-order Large Eddy Simulations to study turbulence. 

I do not just use commercial simulation software; I write the code, understand the underlying mathematics, and validate the physics.
